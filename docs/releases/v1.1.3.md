# Fluval BLE v1.1.3

v1.1.3 corrects the schedule-write verification introduced in v1.1.2.

## Fixed

- Schedule writes are verified with a settle-then-poll readback (0.5 s, 1 s,
  2 s, 2 s) instead of a single immediate read. The AquaSky answers a status
  read issued right after a schedule write with the *previous* schedule and
  reports the new one a second or two later, so v1.1.2 rejected correct writes
  as "did not confirm" (observed live three times on 2026-09-05). A regression
  test replays a stale-then-correct readback sequence.
